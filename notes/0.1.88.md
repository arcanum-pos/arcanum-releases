## New
- **Push printers: trust the printer from the screen.** A screen that can't reach its printer (station screen, waiter's phone, or the kassa's Instellingen → Bonprinter) now says so from the printer button, and shows how to fix it.
  - **On a Mac, Windows or Linux computer:** a command to paste in a terminal. It reads the printer's own certificate, sends it to Arcanum with a one-time code (valid 15 minutes) and trusts it on that computer. No admin rights needed.
  - **On an iPad or iPhone:** a profile to download in Safari, plus the steps to follow in Instellingen. The link is valid 10 minutes.
  - **In the console:** Bewerken on the printer shows the fingerprint of the uploaded certificate.
  - Arcanum warns if the certificate won't be accepted by Apple devices (no address in it, valid longer than 825 days, or marked as a certificate authority).

## Changed
- **The waiter's phone is a personal device.** It is never asked who is working, also when "Medewerker kiezen verplicht" is on. The signed-in account, or the staff member linked to it, is recorded on the orders.
- **The station screen header** wraps neatly on a narrow screen: the printer button and the ? stay together on the right.

## Updating
- Update as usual. The update adds the printers' certificates to the database.
- Nobody has to do anything. Nothing changes for a printer until you use the new trust steps.
