## What's new
- **The test mail says it's a test.** It's still an invitation exactly as
  your members get it, so a checker like mail-tester.com scores the real
  thing. But the subject now starts with "Testmail" and the first paragraph
  says it's only a test, so nobody mistakes it for a real invitation.
- **Cloudflare Email Service: the right token permissions.** To find your
  sending domains, the installer's token needs **Zone · Zone · Read**,
  **Zone · Zone Settings · Read** and **Account · Email Sending · Read**.
  The installer now names all three; before, it missed the last one.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
