## What's new
- **Self-ordering.** Guests order with their phone. In the console, under
  Evenementen → Instellen, give an event its menu and switch on
  self-ordering: you get a link and a QR code to print and put on the
  tables. Guests scan it, choose from the menu, see their cart, and place
  the order. They get a number to say at the toog, where they pay. An
  unpaid order expires after 10 minutes.
- **Short order numbers per event.** Every order of an event gets its own
  number: 1, 2, 3… The kassa shows it ("#5", "Zelfbestelling #5"), and the
  new **#** field in the kassa's tab strip opens an order by its number.
  Numbers can start again at 1 (Evenementen → Instellen).
- **Station screens.** A screen for the kitchen, the bar or any other
  station (Toestellen → Toestel toevoegen → Stationscherm). It shows that
  station's part of every order, in columns: Besteld, In bereiding, Klaar.
  A tap moves an order on, until "Afgeleverd". By default it only shows
  paid orders; a switch shows unpaid ones too, another one the delivered
  ones.
- **Follow your order, live.** After ordering, a guest's phone shows their
  number. It says when the order is being prepared and, with a vibration
  and a sound, when it's ready and where to pick it up. Once everything is
  picked up, it's ready for a new order. Customers who order at the toog
  can follow their order too: after paying, the customer display shows a
  QR code for it.
- Station screens and phones update the moment something changes; nothing
  checks for changes on a timer.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- To use station screens, your products need a station (Producten →
  Stations, then pick it per product). Products without a station never go
  to a station screen.
- Self-ordering works on a phone with the page open. A signal while the
  phone is locked follows later.
