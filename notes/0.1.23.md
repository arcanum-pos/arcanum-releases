## What's new
- Fix: after changing the login provider (for example from Google to
  login.kaboutersoft.be), settings only the old provider needed — like
  Google's separate browser client and scopes — stayed on the Workers and
  broke signing in. The installer now removes settings that are no longer
  needed.
- Fix: accepting an invitation checks again that the sign-in comes from the
  installation's own login provider.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
