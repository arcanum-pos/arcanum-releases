## New
- **The ? button: ask for help or report a problem.** A ? in the top right of every screen your team uses (Hulp on the kassa): the console, the kassa, Instellingen, the station screen, the waiter's phone, Tap to Pay and the start and sign-in screens. It opens the support form of the team behind Arcanum inside the screen, ready to send a bug, an idea or a question.
  - **A screenshot of the screen** is taken when you press ?, and shown in the form. A checkbox decides whether it goes along: look at it first, it may show names or amounts.
  - **Technical details** (which screen, which release, the browser, the last errors) go along to help find the problem. The form lists every one of them under "Technische gegevens die meegaan".
  - **Nothing leaves the device before you press Versturen.** When you're signed in, your e-mail address is filled in for a reply; clear it if you'd rather not get one.
  - **Add files** if they help: up to 5, such as a photo or a screen recording.
- **The kassa: who's working moved** from the top right to the row above the products, next to the search field. Top down: who you are, find a product, tap it.
- **Not on what guests see:** the customer display, self-ordering, the pickup screen and the entrance screen have no ?.
