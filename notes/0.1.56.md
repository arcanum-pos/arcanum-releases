## What's new
- **Tables and waiters.** Waiters take orders at the table on their own
  phone.
  - **Console → Tafels** (new): add your tables, by zone if you like
    ("Zaal", "Terras"). Each table has its own QR code; print them all on
    one page.
  - **Ordering mode → "Aan tafel"** (in the event's settings): waiters
    order from this mode's menu. Turn it on for the mode you use at the
    tables, e.g. "Ter plaatse".
  - **The role Bediening** (Gebruikers → invite): for waiters. They only
    see the waiter screen, not the kassa or the console.
  - **The waiter screen** at `/bediening`, signed in on the waiter's own
    phone. It shows the tables by zone and colour:
    - **Vrij:** "Gasten plaatsen" opens the table's tab.
    - **Bezet:** what's ordered and still to pay; **Bestellen** sends a
      round to the stations; **Afrekenen** with Bancontact (the guest scans
      the QR on the waiter's phone) or a SumUp Solo the waiter carries. Cash
      is paid at the till: the till finds the table's tab under Rekeningen.
    - **Af te ruimen** (after paying): "Opgeruimd" makes it Vrij again.
    - It updates live when something changes elsewhere.
  - **Scanning a table's QR code:** a waiter's phone opens that table on
    the waiter screen. A guest's phone, while the table is Bezet and the
    mode also has "Met de QR-code" on, lets the guests order another round
    on their table's tab themselves.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- Nothing changes until you add tables and turn on "Aan tafel".
