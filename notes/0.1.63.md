## What's new
- **Who worked on a shift.** The kasopmaak on the kassa and the shift list in the console (Kassalades) now show who worked on the shift: everyone who took a payment on it, with how many ("Bert (3) · Barbara (1)"), and whoever opened or closed it or moved cash. The person is the one chosen at the top of the kassa, or else the one signed in.

## Fixes
- **Kasopmaak counts the latest payments.** Opening the kasopmaak could show the totals from before the last sales — only the opening float, after a few cash payments — until the kassa page was reloaded or looked at again. The kassa now fetches the shift's totals when the kasopmaak opens and after every payment. The payments themselves were always booked on the shift correctly.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. No database changes.
