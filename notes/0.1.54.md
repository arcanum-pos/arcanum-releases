## What's new
- **Who's working on a shared kassa or station screen (Medewerkers).**
  Volunteers behind a bar often have no account and share one device. Now
  each of them can be recorded by name.
  - In the console, **Medewerkers** (new, next to Gebruikers): add people
    with a name and, if you like, a PIN of 4 to 6 digits. No account
    needed. Only admins manage this list.
  - On the kassa and the station screen, a new button at the top shows
    **who's working**. Tap it, then your name (and your PIN, if you have
    one). It stays chosen until someone else picks theirs.
  - Every order, payment and tab is recorded with that person, next to the
    account the kassa is signed in with. The Rekeningen overview shows who
    opened a tab.
  - **Medewerker kiezen verplicht** (a switch on the Medewerkers page): a
    kassa then only sells once someone is chosen. Off by default.
- **Station screen: who's making what.**
  - **Start** puts the chosen person's name on the ticket ("Jan maakt
    dit").
  - **Doorgeven…** hands a ticket to someone else.
  - **Terug** to "Besteld" puts it back in the queue, without a name.
  - **Alleen mijn bonnen** hides what colleagues are making; new tickets
    stay visible.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- Nothing changes until you add staff: without them, the kassa and station
  screens work as before.
