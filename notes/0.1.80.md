## New
- **Events over several days.** An event now has a first and a last day (Evenementen → Nieuw evenement, or later under Instellen → Naam en dagen). Leave the last day empty for a one-day event. The list shows the days, for example 12/09/2026 – 13/09/2026.

## Improved
- **Reports follow the chosen event.** Choosing an event sets the period to its days. Buttons let you pick the whole event or one of its days, and the From / To fields only allow dates within the event. Two or three days are charted per hour, a single day per 15 minutes, a longer period per day.
- **One line per item on the bill.** The same item ordered in several rounds is now one line, both on the customer display and on the till's ticket (for example 5 × Bon instead of 2 × Bon and 3 × Bon). Cancelling some of them takes them off the latest round first, and paying per item works as before.
- **Report charts:** the gap between the stacked blocks is now the same everywhere.
- **Installer:** a "Terug naar de console" link at the top right.

## Updating
- Update as usual. One database change (an event's last day); the installer applies it.
