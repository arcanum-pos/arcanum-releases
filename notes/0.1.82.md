## Improved
- **Kassa: one line per product while waiting for the payment.** The same product from several rounds is now one line above the amount (for example 5 × Bon instead of 2 × Bon and 3 × Bon), as on the ticket and the customer display. Paying per item shows price and total per line too.
- **Kassa: groups with quick buttons can be arranged.** In **Indeling aanpassen** there is now a row over the whole width above the columns. A group with quick buttons (for example Bonnen 5, 10, 15) starts there as before, and can now be dragged into a column like any other group. Any group can also be dragged into the full-width row. A till that was already arranged keeps its layout.
- **Kassa: the message "Deze rekening is intussen afgesloten"** (when another kassa closes the rekening you have open) now disappears when you switch to another rekening.
- **The check mark** on the kassa, the Tap to Pay phone and the per-item selection is now straight.
