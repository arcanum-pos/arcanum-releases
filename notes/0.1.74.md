## New
- **The customer display in your own colours.** In the console, Instellingen → Weergave → Klantscherm: upload your logo and pick a main colour and an accent colour — say your scout group's green and its scarf colour, or the club colours. The customer display then shows a header with the logo and the event's name, a stripe in the accent colour, and the total in the main colour; the text on it always stays readable. An event can have colours of its own (Evenementen → Instellen). Only the customer display changes; the kassa and the console keep their look. Without a logo or colours, the display looks as before.
- **No more "Bestelling toevoegen aan rekening".** What you tap appears on the tab straight away, marked as new, and can still be changed. The round goes to the kitchen and bar by itself, as one ticket, when you pay, switch to another tab, open Rekeningen, or after 30 seconds without a tap.
- **Ordering and the tab clearly apart.** The left side of the kassa now says which tab you're ordering for ("Bestellen voor #1 Tafel 4"); the right side shows what's on the tab.
- **The customer display at rest** now has the same layout as while an order is entered: an empty order and € 0,00, ready for the next customer.

## Fixes
- Button text no longer runs over the edge of its button (e.g. on a smaller tablet or in French).
