## Changed
- **The kassa: more room for product names.** A product button shows the name on top (up to three lines, so long names no longer get cut off) and below it how many are on the rekening on the left and the price on the right.
  - **The +1 is gone.** The count says how many the rekening has; it's dark while some of them are new, grey once everything is sent. What's new is listed on the ticket on the right.
- **The kassa: one row above the products.** The rekening you're ordering for, who's working, the search field and Indeling aanpassen sit on one row. The number of items and the total moved off it - they're on the ticket on the right. On a narrow screen the search field goes to a second row; Indeling aanpassen shows just its icon until there's room.
- **The kassa: cancel a line with the ×** in front of it on the ticket, instead of the word Annuleren behind it. It still asks first. A line that's fully paid has no ×.
- **The kassa: the selected rekening** (or Toog) on the left shows its whole outline again.

## Fixed
- Uploading a logo or app icon in the console works in Safari-based browsers on Linux.

## Under the hood
- Every screen tells the browser a little more about what it may do: no camera, microphone, location, payment or USB access, and no other website may keep a handle on an Arcanum window or load its pages as a resource.
- Package updates (React and others). Each release is now also checked automatically: tests in Chrome and Safari's engine, a scan for known weaknesses in the packages, a code scan and a scan of the pages' security settings.

## Updating
