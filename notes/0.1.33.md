## What's new
### Devices are paired with a code
- **A kassa or customer display is added from the console now.**
  Toestellen → **Toestel toevoegen**: choose Kassa or Klantscherm and a
  name. You get a code (valid 10 minutes, works once) and a QR code. On the
  device, open your Arcanum's address and enter the code under **Dit toestel
  koppelen**, or scan the QR code. Nobody turns a laptop into a kassa by
  accident any more: the old "Wat is dit toestel?" choice is gone.
- **A customer display for a given kassa**: when making its code, choose
  "Voor welke kassa?" — the display is linked as soon as it's paired.
- **The start page** (your Arcanum's address): **Dit toestel koppelen** or
  **Beheer** (the console). A paired device opens its screen right away.
- **Names everywhere**: Toestellen, and the kassa's Instellingen when
  linking a customer display, show "Kassa toog" instead of a technical id.
  A name saved in the kassa's Instellingen shows in Toestellen too.
- **For admins, in the kassa's Instellingen**: **Beheer (console)** (opens
  in a new tab) and **Dit toestel ontkoppelen**.
- **"Klantscherm openen"** on the kassa keeps using the same customer
  display, instead of adding a new one to Toestellen every time.

### Fixed
- **The customer display now shows a SumUp payment on a reader as paid.**
  It stayed on "waiting for your payment" while the kassa already showed it
  paid.
- **Only members of an organisation can see or change its devices.** Before,
  anyone signed in to your Arcanum could register, list or remove devices of
  any organisation on it. No payment or sales data was reachable that way.

### Smaller
- Someone signed in who isn't a member of any organisation sees "Je bent
  geen lid van een organisatie" with what to do, instead of an empty console.
- Settings → Payment providers says what SumUp and Bancontact need (for real
  payments and for testing), with a link to the guide in the manual.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. Two
  database changes, applied by the installer.
- **Your existing kassa's and customer displays keep working** — nothing to
  re-pair. They appear in Toestellen without a name until you give them one
  in their Instellingen.
- **Reload the kassa and customer display screens that are open** while you
  update (or restart those devices), so they pick up the new version.
- The extra customer displays that "Klantscherm openen" added before this
  version stay in Toestellen, unlinked; you can remove them there.
