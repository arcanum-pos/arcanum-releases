## What's new
- **One sign-in page, two ways in.** Opening your Arcanum's address while
  signed out now offers both: **Aanmelden** on this device (password,
  Google or passkey), or **Met je telefoon** — scan the QR code, sign in on
  your phone, and the device follows. Nobody needs to know the /device
  address any more (it still works).
- **The QR code renews itself** before it expires, so a kassa or customer
  display can wait on the sign-in page all day without showing an error.
- **After signing in you land where you were going**, also when signing in
  with your phone — a pairing code's link included, so a new tablet goes
  from signing in straight to pairing.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
