## What's new
- **The customer display shows the order while it's being entered.** As the cashier taps items in, the customer sees each line, the quantities and the total, with "Klopt je bestelling?" — so they can say whether it's right before the cashier asks for the payment. Pressing Afrekenen switches to the payment screen as before; after "Bedankt!" the display rests for the next customer. Works for a display in the kassa's own second window and on another device.
- **"Bestelling tonen" on the kassa.** A new toggle in the kassa's header (per kassa, remembered) switches that live view off — for instance while the cashier checks open tabs. It only affects the order being entered: a payment always shows every detail.
- **App icons.** Every screen now has its own icon when added to a home screen, Dock or start menu (iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows) and in the browser tab — no more grey square with a letter. Kassa, Klantscherm, Station, Bediening, Tap to Pay, Bestellen and the console each look different; Instellingen uses the kassa's.
- **Your own icons (Instellingen → Weergave → App-iconen).** Admins can upload an organisation's own icon for each of those pages, for the console, and for each prep station (Keuken, Bar, …) separately. One image is enough — every size is made from it; Verwijderen goes back to the default.
- **Install a device as an app, then pair it there.** The pairing link and QR from Toestellen → Toestel toevoegen now carry the device's name and icon. In Safari (iPhone, iPad, Mac) the pairing page asks first: **Als app** (with the steps for that device — the app then pairs itself, so it keeps its pairing) or **In deze browser**. In Chrome, Edge and Android it pairs right away and the screen offers to install itself. A device installed this way opens straight to its screen every time. Toestel toevoegen also has **Kopieer link**, and — when the console runs in Safari on a Mac — **Open op deze computer**, handy for running several devices as separate Dock apps on one Mac.
- **Opnieuw koppelen (re-pair a device).** In Toestellen, every device has **Opnieuw koppelen**: a code that makes a browser that same device again — after its storage was cleared, on a new tablet, or to move it into an installed app. It keeps its name, its link to a kassa or customer display, its station and its history in the reports. Whichever browser was that device before stops being it and says so.
- **Guests' order page: no more reopening a finished order.** An order page added to a phone's home screen (for notifications) used to reopen the same order every time. Once that order is collected and paid, it now opens the event's menu instead — or the guest's newer order, if they placed one — and stays inside the app. A next order gets notifications without asking again.

## Fixes
- **Printing table QR codes.** The printout contained the whole console and the QR codes overlapped. Now only the cards are printed, sized for A4: 12 per page (3 across, 4 down), 42 mm QR codes, never split across pages.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. Three small database additions (re-pairing, custom icons, the customer display's live view) are applied automatically during the update.
- Already-installed home-screen or Dock apps keep their old icon until they're removed and added again.
