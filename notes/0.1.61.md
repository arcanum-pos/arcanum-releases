## What's new
- **Tap to Pay at the kassa.** A kassa without a SumUp reader can take cards through the SumUp app on a phone.
  - **Set up once:** in the console, Toestellen → Toestel toevoegen → **Tap to Pay-telefoon**, and choose its kassa. Open the code on that phone (it opens the phone's own page) and turn on notifications there. On an iPhone, first add the page to the home screen and pair from there — the page shows how. The phone needs the SumUp app, signed in, with Tap to Pay set up; the SumUp affiliate key must be filled in under Betaalproviders.
  - **At the kassa:** choose the new payment method **Tap to Pay**. The kassa's phone gets the payment straight away (a notification when it's locked); one tap opens the SumUp app with the amount, the customer taps their card, and the kassa shows "Betaald".
  - **A QR code on the kassa** opens the same payment on any phone with the SumUp app — handy when the kassa's phone isn't at hand.
  - **Only SumUp confirms the payment** — there is no "confirm" at the kassa. Cancelling at the kassa checks with SumUp first: if the customer had just paid, it stays paid.
