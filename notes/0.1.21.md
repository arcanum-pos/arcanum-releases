## What's new
### Security fixes
- Payments and the sales ledger: only an admin or cashier of the
  organization can start, confirm or follow a payment, list its card
  readers, or record a sale.
- Signing in: protection against login forgery, no more bearer-token
  sign-in, sessions stored encrypted, a stricter session cookie, and the
  sign-in screens no longer use storage before someone actually signs in.
- Fewer random sign-outs when the session is renewed.
- Every screen sends security headers (no framing by other sites, HSTS).
- The installer behind Arcanum refuses an administrator address the login
  provider marks as unverified.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes. Nobody is signed out; a sign-in that's in progress at
  the moment of the update has to be started again.
