## New
- **Invited people can always sign in with login.kaboutersoft.be.** On login.kaboutersoft.be, only people who were given access to an installation can sign in to it; anyone else is offered "Request access", and the installation's owner decides. Arcanum now gives that access itself: whoever you invite in the console (or brings an imported organisation with them) can sign in straight away, and loses access once they have no membership left on your installation. Members from before this version are given access automatically the first time an admin opens the console after the update.
- **Your admins decide on access requests.** Everyone on your installation's admin list (the installer's "Beheerders") and everyone who is an admin of one of your organisations is now also an admin of your installation at login.kaboutersoft.be: they get the mail when someone asks for access, and approve or refuse it there. Whoever installed it stays one too.

## Updating
- Update as usual. No database changes. The installer now also gives the backend your installation's login client id and secret; nothing to fill in.
- With another login provider than login.kaboutersoft.be, nothing changes.
