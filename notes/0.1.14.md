## What's new

### Change the login provider without locking anyone out
Moving your installation to another login provider (say from
login.kaboutersoft.be to your own Google Workspace or Microsoft) is now a
guided, safe step in the installer, under **Geavanceerd → Aanmelding
wijzigen**:

1. Enter the new provider. It's checked, but nothing changes yet. The page
   shows which addresses to register at the new provider, and who will
   have to sign in again.
2. **Test-aanmelding** — sign in at the new provider once. The installer
   shows what came back (your e-mail address, and whether it's verified).
3. **Toepassen** — you're linked to the new provider straight away, so you
   can't lock yourself out. Everyone else signs in again with the new
   provider and gets their role back automatically, matched on e-mail
   address.
4. Changed your mind? **Terugzetten** puts everything back as it was, for
   7 days. Your recovery code works throughout.

Choosing a provider during the first install works as before.

### One login provider and one address per installation
The console no longer has the per-organisation **Huisstijl** (own domain)
and **Aanmelding** (own login provider) pages: both belong to the
installation now, and are set in the installer under Geavanceerd. Login
always uses the installation's own address.

### Safer invitations
An invitation is only accepted on an e-mail address that the login
provider hasn't marked as unverified.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
- If you had set a custom domain or login provider **per organisation**
  (on the old shared platform), move it to the installation in the
  installer before updating.
