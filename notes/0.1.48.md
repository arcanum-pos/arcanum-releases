## What's new
- **Ordering modes at the kassa: one switch, off by default.** Whether the
  kassa labels its orders with an ordering mode is now one switch for the
  whole event, in Evenementen → Instellen: "Bestelwijze kiezen aan de
  kassa". It replaces the "Aan de kassa" switch each mode had.
  - Off (the default): the kassa asks nothing and its orders get no label.
    That's right when you use separate products per ordering mode, each
    with its own station and price.
  - On: the kassa shows the ordering modes as buttons, and the one chosen
    shows on the station screen (for example "Take-away", so the kitchen
    knows to pack it). Useful with one station and shared products.
  - The kassa keeps its own menu.
- **"Gasten bestellen zelf" can always be switched on.** If the event
  has no ordering mode with a menu yet, Arcanum sets one up for you: a mode
  "Ter plaatse" with your menu, or QR ordering switched on for your first
  mode. You adjust it afterwards. Only an organisation without any menu
  sees "Maak eerst een menukaart".
- **Event settings, tidied up:**
  - Every setting now looks the same: a title with its switch on the
    right, a short explanation underneath, and a line between settings.
  - You add an ordering mode from the dropdown ("+ Bestelwijze toevoegen")
    instead of the box at the bottom.
  - The guests' link and the direct links show in full, and a direct link
    is shorter now. Direct links you shared before keep working.
- **Installer, Sms:** a sender name that's too long now says why (at most
  11 characters), and the "Test SMS to" field looks like the other fields.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- After updating, the switch is off for every event, so the kassa no longer
  shows ordering-mode buttons. Switch it on in Evenementen → Instellen
  where you want them.
- Reload the kassas after updating.
