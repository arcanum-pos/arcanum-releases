## What's new

### The installer moves into Arcanum by itself
After installing, there's nothing left to do:
- The installer is linked to Arcanum as soon as Arcanum is installed (or
  with this update, for an existing installation). From then on it's at
  `https://<your Arcanum>/installer/`, for administrators who are signed in.
- The first time you click **Open je Arcanum**, the installer switches its
  own public address (`arcanum-installer.….workers.dev`) off, then opens the
  console. You might see the installer for a moment on the way.
- After that, **Open je Arcanum** just opens the console.

The separate "Openbare toegang verwijderen" section is gone. You can switch
the installer's own address back on under **Geavanceerd → Toegang tot de
installer**. It then stays on until you switch it off there again. While
you're changing the login provider (Aanmelding wijzigen), it's switched on
automatically, because the test sign-in and the recovery code only work
there.

### One way to install: start.kaboutersoft.be
The installer always shows the simple screen now: **Installeren**, with
everything else under **Geavanceerd**. The "Deploy to Cloudflare" button and
the installer password (`INSTALLER_PASSWORD`) are gone. You sign in with your
account, or with your recovery code.

## Updating
- Installed through start.kaboutersoft.be: nothing to do. The installer
  updates itself first, then Arcanum. No database changes.
- **Installed with the "Deploy to Cloudflare" button: don't update.** The new
  installer no longer has the password sign-in, so you'd be locked out of
  your installer (Arcanum itself keeps working). Instead, export your data,
  delete the installation (its Workers, databases and KV namespaces,
  including `arcanum-installer` and its KV), and install again through
  start.kaboutersoft.be.
- Only the installer changed in this release; the other components are the
  same as in 0.1.15.
