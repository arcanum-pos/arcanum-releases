## What's new
- **A notification when the order is ready.** A guest following their order
  on their phone can now get a notification as soon as a station has their
  part ready, even with the screen locked. One notification per station
  ("Je bestelling #12 is klaar - Keuken."). It's free: no SMS costs.
  - **Android (and computers):** on the order page, tap **"Melding
    aanzetten"** and allow notifications.
  - **iPhone:** Apple only allows notifications for a page on the home
    screen. The order page shows how, in three steps: Deel → Zet op
    beginscherm → open "Bestelling" from the home screen and turn it on
    there. The icon opens that same order.
  - **No double messages:** a guest with notifications on gets no SMS. If
    the phone no longer accepts notifications, the SMS still goes when the
    guest left a number.
  - Not for tables served by waiters: the waiter brings the order.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer). The installer
  also creates the installation's own notification key; you don't need to
  do anything.
