## What's new
- Signing in no longer asks the backend for the login provider's client
  secret: the front Worker has its own copy, set by the installer.
- **Aanmelding wijzigen → "Terug naar login.kaboutersoft.be"**: an
  installation made through start.kaboutersoft.be whose Arcanum uses another
  login provider can move to its own login.kaboutersoft.be client in one
  click — nothing to type. As always: test sign-in first, everyone else
  signs in again once, and undo is possible for 7 days. Kassas and customer
  displays need the QR login once after the switch.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
