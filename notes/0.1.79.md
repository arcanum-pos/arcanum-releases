## Improved
- **Reports: one event filter for the whole page.** The event choice now sits at the top, next to Vandaag / Gisteren / Deze week / Deze maand / Aangepast. Pick one event, or **Alle evenementen** for everything in that period. It narrows the totals, the tables by payment method, category, product and VAT, the charts and the payment list alike.
- **Reports: two charts.** **Aantal betalingen** (the number of payments) and **Bedrag van de betalingen** (their amount), each stacked by payment method (Bancontact, Contant, SumUp). A single day is shown per 15 minutes, from the first payment's hour to the last one's. A week, a month or a longer custom period is shown per day. Hover a bar for the numbers per method and its total.
