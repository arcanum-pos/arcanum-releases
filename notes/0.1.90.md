## New
- **Cash at the kassa: a change calculator.** On the cash screen, fill in "Ontvangen" (what the customer hands over), by typing it or with the quick note buttons. The screen shows "Terug te geven" and in which notes and coins to give it, or "Nog € x te weinig" when it isn't enough. Filling it in is optional: "Bevestig ontvangst contant geld" works as before.
- **Keep the change as a tip.** When the customer says "keep the change", press "Betaald, € x is fooi" instead of "Betaald, € x terug". The amount is added as a tip to that payment, so the receipt, the reports and the kasopmaak include it.
