## What's new
- **Know who's behind a guest's tab.** For ordering modes where guests
  order on a tab and pay when they leave, you can now ask for a name and a
  mobile number or e-mail when they open the tab. Switch it on per ordering
  mode in Evenementen → Instellen ("Naam en gsm-nummer of e-mail vragen").
  - The name becomes the tab's name at the kassa, for example "#6 Jan".
  - The cashier sees the number and e-mail in the tab, so you have
    something to go on if a tab is left unpaid.
  - The details aren't verified, so guests don't need an account or a
    code.
  - They're erased as soon as the tab is paid or cancelled.
  - If the ordering mode sends "ready" SMS, the number given here gets
    them too.
- Paying once at the end costs a single Bancontact fee per tab instead of
  one per round.
- **Menu import: products with the same name.** Two products may now share
  a name in different categories, for example "Bolognese" under Take away
  and under Ter plaatse, as the console already allowed. An exported menu
  now imports back unchanged.
  - A row with an empty Product cell still belongs to the product above.
  - When a name appears in two categories, every row that starts that
    product again must fill in its category.
  - Products you renamed to get the import through (like "Bolognese_")
    can get their own name back in the console. Fixing the name in the sheet
    instead would make new products and leave the old ones off the menu.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- Reload the kassas after updating, so they show the details.
