## What's new
- **A readable self-order link.** The link and QR code of an event are now
  easy to type over, for example `/bestel/spaghetti-7kq2`: the event's name
  and 4 random characters. You can change the name in Evenementen →
  Instellen.
  - The 4 characters keep strangers from guessing the link and ordering
    from home.
  - QR codes you already printed keep working until you make a new link.
- **Guests no longer lose their order page.** As soon as an order is
  placed, the address becomes the order's own page. Back and Forward, a
  reload and coming back from Bancontact all land on it, and scanning the
  event's QR again brings it back too.
- **A new order asks "Ter plaatse or Take away?" again**, also after paying
  with Bancontact.
- The "Bewaar deze link" box is gone, since the browser now remembers the
  order. It only shows when a browser keeps nothing at all.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- After updating, check your event's new link in Evenementen → Instellen.
  Print the new QR code whenever it suits you; the old one keeps working.
