## What's new
- **Korting: products with a negative price.** Give a product a negative
  price on a menu (e.g. "Korting lid" at -1,50), in the console or in an
  imported menu sheet. At the kassa it's a button like any other: tapping
  it lowers the tab's total. Amounts below zero read "-€ 1,50".
- How it behaves:
  - A tab is only paid for what's still outstanding, so a discount can
    never pay money out.
  - A tab that ends at exactly € 0,00 because of a discount can be
    cancelled.
  - One that goes below zero can't be paid: cancel the discount line first.
  - Reports show the discount as a product with negative revenue. Give it
    the VAT rate of what it discounts, so the VAT totals stay right.
