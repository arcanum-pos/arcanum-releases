## What's new
- **Test keys are flagged under Betaalproviders.**
  - A Bancontact key for the test environment (preprod) shows **Test
    (preprod)** on its card. When you confirm a test key, the console
    explains that Bancontact answers with its own test merchant there, not
    your organisation. A production key shows your own name and account.
  - A SumUp sandbox account shows **Test (sandbox)** on its card, and when
    you confirm it, as SumUp's merchant profile says. SumUp keys saved
    before this release get the label once you set them again.
- **Setting a Bancontact key again keeps its environment.** The dialog now
  starts on the environment the key was saved for, instead of always on
  production.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes in this release.
