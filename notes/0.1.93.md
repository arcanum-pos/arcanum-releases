## New
- **Install as an app in Chrome, Edge and Android.** These browsers now offer to install the kassa, the other device screens and the console as an app by themselves. Before, only the general "install this site" option in the browser menu worked there.
- **"Installeer als app" catches the browser's offer sooner.** After pairing, the card now picks up the browser's offer from the moment the page loads. If the browser has not made an offer after 45 seconds, the card explains the steps in the browser menu (Edge, Chrome and Android).

## Changed
- **The install card sits in the middle of the kassa screen.** In the corner it covered "Afrekenen" and "Op rekening". On the other screens it stays in the corner.
- **"Aan de slag" on the Overzicht: Kassalade step.** The card now stays until the Kassalade step is done too. A club without cash can choose "Niet nodig" to skip that step. The X still hides the whole card.
- **Cash: the kassa suggests what a customer hands over.** The buttons for the amount received showed only the next single notes, so for € 74 only € 100, and above € 100 none at all. Now they show the amount rounded up to each note: for € 74 that is € 75, € 80 and € 100 (50+20+5, 50+20+10 or 4 × 20, a € 100 note). At most four buttons, also above € 100.

## Fixed
- **No more translation offer on the staff screens.** On a computer set to English, Edge offered to translate the kassa "from Dutch", which can break the screen. The staff screens already come in Dutch, French and English, so the offer is gone there. Self-ordering stays translatable, so a guest can have the menu translated on their own phone.

## Updating
- Update as usual. The update does not change the database.
- Nobody has to do anything. A device that is already installed keeps working as before.
