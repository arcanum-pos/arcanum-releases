## Fixes
- **Cancelling a payment really cancels it.** On the payment screen, "Terug naar rekening" is now **Betaling annuleren**. For Bancontact the QR stops working, and for SumUp the payment disappears from the reader. The tab is free again right away, without a lingering "Er loopt een betaling" message, and the customer display goes back to waiting. If the customer had just paid, it stays paid. If Bancontact or SumUp can't be reached, the kassa says so and you can try again (the payment also expires on its own).
- **Reloading the kassa no longer leaves the customer display behind.** A kassa that starts up sets its customer display back to waiting.
- **Pairing a SumUp reader** accepts the code with dashes and in other lengths (e.g. a virtual reader's code from SumUp's dashboard); SumUp itself says whether the code is right.
- **The menu no longer shifts when you tap.** Products keep their place when their counts change, and the search field keeps its width when you pick a group.
- **Arranging the groups is clearer.** The group you drag follows your finger, the column where it will land lights up, and a line shows exactly where it goes. The whole column accepts it, and dropping in a gap goes to the nearest column instead of snapping back.
