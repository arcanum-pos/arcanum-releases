## New
- **Ticket printers.** A receipt printer in the kitchen or at the bar prints the tickets of its station as they come in. Epson printers for now (Star later).
  - **Added in the console:** Toestellen → Toestel toevoegen → Printer. Choose the stations whose tickets it prints and the paper (80 or 58 mm).
  - **The printer fetches its own work** (recommended): it gets a link to put in its own settings (Server Direct Print), with the steps shown. No screen needed next to it, and nothing to install on any device. The list shows when it last asked. The link can be found again under Bewerken; "Nieuwe link" replaces it.
  - **Or via a screen on the same network:** the station screen sends the tickets to the printer's address. Works in Chrome only, after accepting the printer's certificate once.
  - **Every ticket prints once,** however many screens are open. Out of paper or the cover open: the station screen says so, and "Opnieuw" prints it again.
  - **The station screen** has a printer button in its header: which printer, how it's doing, a test page, and what didn't print.
- **Receipts at the kassa and in table service,** switched on per organisation (Toestellen → "Bonnen printen"; off by default).
  - **At the kassa:** the printer is chosen in Instellingen → Bonprinter, optionally "after every payment, by itself". After a payment, "Bon printen".
  - **In table service:** the phone chooses its printer in the header; "Bon printen" once the table has paid.
  - **On the receipt:** the organisation, the event, the rekening, the lines, the total and how it was paid. **Not a VAT receipt** - it says "Geen BTW-kasticket" at the bottom.

## Which printers
- **Recommended:** an Epson TM-m30III or TM-T88VI/VII with Ethernet or Wi-Fi.
- **Not usable:** a USB-only version of any model (check that the printer has a network connection), and Epson's mobile printers.

## Updating
- Update as usual. The update adds the printers, their print jobs and the receipts switch to the database.
- Nothing changes until a printer is added.
- A printer that fetches its own work asks about 8,640 times a day at the recommended 10 seconds: about 9% of what Cloudflare's free plan allows per day.
