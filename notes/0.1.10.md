## What's new

### Several kassas, one set of rekeningen — live
Rekeningen always belonged to the organisation, shared by every kassa — but a
kassa only noticed another kassa's changes when it was used again. Now every
change reaches every kassa of the organisation the moment it happens:

- A rekening opened, ordered on, renamed, split or cancelled on one kassa shows
  up on all the others right away, including the rekening that's open there.
- A payment starting, succeeding, failing or expiring does too ("Betaling
  loopt…" in the tab strip).
- A rekening that was paid on another kassa while it was open here says so, and
  this kassa goes back to Toog.
- A new **Vernieuwen** button at the end of the tab strip reloads everything by
  hand, for the rare push that doesn't arrive.

### Rekeningen: today's overview
A new **Rekeningen** button in the kassa header shows every rekening of today,
from every kassa: opened or closed since midnight, plus any still open.

- Per rekening: time, name, kassa, number of items, payment method(s), status
  (Open, Betaling loopt, Betaald, Geannuleerd) and amount — newest first.
- Filter by status, search by name, `#number` or amount (`41,50`), and a
  running total of what's been paid and what's still open.
- Tap an open rekening to continue it on this kassa. A paid or cancelled one
  opens read-only: its lines, its payments, its receipt number, and when and
  where it was opened and closed.
- The list updates live as other kassas work, in Dutch, French and English,
  light and dark.

"Today" becomes "this shift" once shifts are kept on the server. Refunds and
reprinting a receipt are not part of this release.

## Updating
- The update adds one database index (migration `0021_charges_tab_index`,
  applied by the installer). No data changes.
- Kassas and customer displays reconnect by themselves after the update; no
  need to register them again.
