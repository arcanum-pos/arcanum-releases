## What's new
- **Tap to Pay on the waiter's phone.** At the table, the guest pays by
  card (or phone) against the waiter's own phone, through the SumUp
  Business app.
  - **Set it up once:**
    - In SumUp, go to Instellingen → For Developers → Toolkit → Affiliate
      Keys. Add an app id there (e.g. `be.mijnclub.kassa`).
    - In the console, go to Instellingen → Betaalproviders → SumUp → "Tap
      to Pay instellen". Enter the key (`sup_afk_…`) and that app id.
  - **On the waiter's phone:** the SumUp Business app, signed in to the
    same SumUp account, with Tap to Pay set up in the app (on an iPhone:
    Instellingen → Tap to Pay op iPhone).
  - **Paying:** Afrekenen → **Tap to Pay (SumUp-app)**. The SumUp app opens
    with the amount; the guest holds their card against the phone. The app
    then returns to the table on the waiter screen.
  - **Checked with SumUp:** a payment only counts once SumUp itself
    confirms it, with the right amount. If the waiter screen doesn't come
    back, the payment is still picked up within a minute.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest.
- Nothing changes until you enter an affiliate key.
