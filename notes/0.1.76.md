## New
- **Make the tab panel as wide as you like.** The line between ordering and the tab can be dragged: wider for long product names on the tab, narrower for more room on the menu. A double-click puts it back. Each kassa keeps its own width.
- **Arrange the menu's groups yourself.** With "Alles" selected, tap **Indeling aanpassen**, drag each group by its grip to another place or column (or add a column), and tap **Klaar**. Each kassa keeps its own arrangement, per menu — screens differ. "Standaardindeling" goes back to the automatic layout. While arranging, products can't be tapped by accident.
