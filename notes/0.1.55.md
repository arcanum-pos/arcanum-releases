## What's new
- **Kassalades, Kassa openen and kasopmaak.** A kassa can now work with a
  cash drawer, and open and close it.
  - In the console, **Kassalades** (new): make the drawers, e.g. "Toog".
    Several kassas can share one drawer.
  - On the kassa, **Instellingen → Kassalade**: choose this kassa's drawer.
  - **Kassa openen:** before the first sale, count the change in the
    drawer, as one amount or per note and coin. The next time it suggests
    what was left in the drawer.
  - While it's open, the header shows "Toog · shift 3 · open sinds 18:00",
    with two new buttons:
    - **Kasbeweging:** money in (extra change) or out (to the safe, an
      expense), with a reason.
    - **Kasopmaak:** count everything in the drawer per note and coin. You
      see what should be there (the change at opening, the cash payments
      including tips, and the cash movements) and the difference. A
      difference of €1 or more needs a short note. Then choose how much
      stays in the drawer for the next shift.
  - **Blind tellen** (a switch on the Kassalades page): the expected amount
    only shows after the count.
  - A drawer can't be closed while a payment on it is still under way: the
    kasopmaak waits until it's finished. Open tabs simply carry on in the
    next shift.
  - Signing out with the drawer still open asks: do the kasopmaak first, or
    leave it open for whoever comes next.
  - The Kassalades page lists the latest shifts with their difference.
- **A kassa without a kassalade works exactly as before.** Nothing changes
  until you choose one in Instellingen.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
