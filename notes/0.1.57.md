## What's new
- **Table management** on the waiter screen (`/bediening`).
  - **At a glance:** each Bezet table shows how many minutes the guests
    have been seated, and a dot while something is still being prepared.
  - **Verplaatsen:** the guests move to a free table; their tab, rounds
    and tickets go with them. The table they left becomes Af te ruimen.
  - **Samenvoegen:** two tables' guests become one table: the orders of
    this table go onto the other table's tab. Not possible once something
    has been paid on either tab. This table becomes Af te ruimen.
  - **Beloven:** promise a Bezet or Af te ruimen table to guests waiting at
    the entrance. Seating guests at it clears the promise.
- **The entrance screen ("Tafelscherm (ingang)")** for waiting guests:
  "Tafel 5 — even geduld", then "Tafel 5 — klaar — welkom!" with a chime
  as soon as the table is cleared. Two ways to open it:
  - **As a device:** Console → Toestellen → Toestel toevoegen → type
    "Tafelscherm (ingang)", then pair a screen at the entrance with the
    code.
  - **From the waiter screen:** the "Ingangsscherm" button opens it in a
    second window, e.g. on a screen connected to a laptop.
  - The sound is off until you tap the speaker icon on the screen
    (browsers require a tap).

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- Nothing changes until you use tables (release 0.1.56).
