## What's new
- **An SMS when an order is ready.** Guests can leave their mobile number
  on their order page. When a station sets its part of the order to
  "Klaar", they get an SMS, for example "Scouts Kabouterland: je bestelling
  #12 is klaar - Keuken." Each station sends its own. This also works for
  orders placed at the kassa, through the order page behind the customer
  display's QR code.
  - Only for paid orders, or orders on a tab: an unpaid self-order never
    sends an SMS.
  - Never twice for the same part, even if a station taps "Klaar" again.
  - The number is only used for this. It's erased once the order is ready,
    and after a day at most.
- **Setting it up:**
  1. Create a [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com) account; you pay Twilio for
     every SMS.
  2. In the installer, go to Geavanceerd → **Sms** and enter the Account
     SID, the auth token and a sender (a name of up to 11 characters, or a
     Twilio number). You also choose which countries' numbers are allowed
     (Belgium by default) and a daily maximum (300 by default). The
     installer checks the details with Twilio, and you can send a test SMS.
  3. Then switch on "Sms als het klaar is" per ordering mode, in
     Evenementen → Instellen.
  - Tip: in Twilio, also limit "Geo permissions" to the same countries.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- The Sms section appears in the installer after it has updated itself.
- Reload the kassas, station screens and customer displays after updating.
