## New
- **Order sheet per menu.** In the console, under Menukaarten, the row menu has "Bestelblad afdrukken". It prints a paper sheet for the waiters: the groups and items in the same order as the till, no prices, and a big box next to every item for tally marks. The format is A5, two on one A4 in landscape.
- **Split: several parts in one payment.** When you split a bill equally (for example in 11), one customer can now pay for more than one part at once: pay 3, then 2, and so on. The kassa shows which parts a payment covers, such as "deel 2-4 van 11".
- **Kasoverzicht on the kassa.** While a shift is open, "Meer" shows the Kasoverzicht: the float, the cash paid, every cash movement with the time, the reason and who did it, and what should be in the drawer now.
- **Rekeningen: partly paid bills.** A bill that is partly paid now shows "Deels betaald" with the amount already paid. It appears under both Open and Betaald, and the paid part counts in the totals.

## Changed
- **The kassa makes far fewer requests to the server.** It no longer contacts the customer display when no display is linked on another device, it no longer reloads changes it made itself, and it refreshes at a calm pace when you return to the screen. After a lost connection it catches up again by itself. On a replay of a real busy day this went from about 92 to about 33 requests per bill for the kassa's work. Nothing looks different on the screen.

## Fixed
- **SumUp: confirming by hand after a failed payment.** When the card reader had already reported a failure, confirming by hand still showed "Betaald". It now says that nothing was paid and asks you to cancel and try again.

## Updating
