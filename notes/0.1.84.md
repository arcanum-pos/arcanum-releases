## New
- **A new kassa screen.** The kassa is laid out in three columns, so everything for the next customer is in view at once:
  - **Left: the open rekeningen.** One search field for a name or a number (a guest's #12), the Toog at the top, each rekening with its items, the time it was opened and its amount, and "+ Nieuwe rekening" at the bottom. A round not sent yet shows as "2 nieuw".
  - **Middle: the menu.** Each group is a box in its own colour, with its products as tiles. The tiles show how many are already on the rekening (3×) and what is being added (+1).
  - **Right: the ticket, ready to pay.** The total, the payment method, the tip and Afrekenen stay in view; long rekeningen scroll inside the ticket.
- **A calmer header.** Only Klantscherm, Aanpassen, Bestelling tonen, **Meer** and who is working. Rekeningen, Kasbeweging, Vernieuwen, Kasopmaak, Instellingen and Uitloggen are under **Meer**. On a wide screen (1280 px and more) the menu's groups sit in the middle of the header.
- **Aanpassen: each kassa its own look.** Theme, language, product buttons (Groot, Compact or Lijst), the ticket on the right or the left, text size, and whether the counts and the tip buttons show. Kept on the device; **Standaard** puts everything back.
- **Group colours.** Each menu group gets a colour automatically. To choose one yourself: Menukaarten → a menu → the group's "…" → **Kleur**.

## Improved
- **Paying no longer hides the kassa.** The payment (QR code, cash, card reader) shows on top of the kassa. The flow is the same as before.
- **The ticket's buttons:** Splitsen and Naam wijzigen (or Op rekening zetten on the Toog) as buttons; Lege rekening sluiten and Ticket annuleren are under the ticket's "…", in red.
- **Cancelling a line** counts with − and +, and every kassa dialog has bigger buttons for a finger at the bar.

## Updating
- Update as usual. One database change (a group's colour); the installer applies it.
- Each kassa starts with the new default look: compact tiles, the ticket on the right. Change it per kassa under **Aanpassen**.
