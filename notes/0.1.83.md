## Improved
- **Kassa: arranging in rows and columns.** **Indeling aanpassen** now works with rows, each with its own columns (one column is the whole width). Add a row with **+ Rij** and a column to a row with **+ Kolom**. Drag any group into any row and column, also the group with quick buttons, and back where it was.
- **Empty columns stay until you remove them.** An empty column now keeps its room, also after **Klaar**, so you can leave space on purpose. Remove an empty column with **Kolom verwijderen** and an empty row with **Rij verwijderen**.
- A till that was already arranged keeps its layout.
