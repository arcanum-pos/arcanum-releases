## What's new
- **Event settings fit on the screen.** Evenementen → Instellen is now a
  wide window with two columns: self-ordering, the link, the QR code and
  the order numbers on the left, and the ordering modes on the right. On a
  phone they stack.
- **One ordering mode at a time.** You choose which mode to set up from a
  dropdown, instead of seeing every mode as a separate block. A mode you've
  just added opens straight away.

## Updating
- Update as usual. Only the console changes; there's nothing to reload on
  the kassas.
