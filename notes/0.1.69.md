## What's new
- **Ticket annuleren (cancel a whole ticket).** Tapped Afrekenen by mistake, or a customer who walks away? After going back to the tab, the kassa now has **Ticket annuleren**: pick one reason ("Verkeerd aangeslagen", "Klant annuleert", …) and every item on the ticket is cancelled with it, and the ticket is closed as cancelled — no more cancelling item by item. Nothing is erased: every line and its cancellation stay in the history, exactly as if each line had been cancelled by hand. Available while nothing has been paid on the ticket and no payment is running (a Bancontact payment stays open until it fails or expires, since the customer could still scan the QR).

## Fixes
- **Tap to Pay pairing code: Kopieer link.** Like every other device, a Tap to Pay phone's pairing code now has **Kopieer link**, so the phone can get its link by message instead of scanning the QR. The link carries the organisation's own Tap to Pay icon.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then the rest. No database changes.
