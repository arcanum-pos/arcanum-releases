## What's new
- **Check how well your mail arrives.** In the installer, under
  Geavanceerd → E-mail, you can now send the test mail to another address
  than your own. Paste the address that
  [mail-tester.com](https://www.mail-tester.com) gives you, send, and see
  your score there: whether your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC are in order,
  whether the sending server is on a blocklist, and whether the mail looks
  like spam.
- **The test mail is a real invitation**, from your first organisation and
  in its language, exactly as your members get it. So the score is about
  the mail that matters.
- At most 10 test mails an hour.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
