## New
- **Customer display: paid on the same screen.** Once a payment is confirmed, the bill stays where it was and the confirmation takes the place of the QR code: a green check that draws itself and a green light, like the orange one while waiting. A tap still moves the kassa on to the next customer.
- **Customer display: items per category.** Under the number of items, the display shows how many there are per category (for example 5 Drank · 1 Eten), the same categories as in the reports. Items without a category are counted as Andere.
- **Menukaarten: archiving can be undone.** Turn on **Toon gearchiveerd** to see archived menus again and put one back with **Terugzetten**.

## Improved
- **Console: one "…" menu per line.** Every list (menus, products, categories, stations, events, devices, staff, tables, users, kassalades) now has its actions in a "…" menu at the end of the line, as words. Deleting and archiving are at the bottom, in red. No more trash icons.
- **Devices: status at a glance.** Online is green, offline orange. A SumUp reader is removed with **Verwijderen**, like any other device (it is unpaired at SumUp too).
- **Printing table QR codes** now uses a separate print page. On some computers the console tab went blank and could no longer be used while the print dialog was open.
- **Texts:** long dashes are replaced by a normal dash everywhere.

## Updating
- Update as usual. No database changes.
