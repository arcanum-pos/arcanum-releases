## What's new

### The installer speaks French and English
The installer is now available in Dutch, French and English: every screen,
step, check and error message. It follows your browser's language. You can
switch any time with **NL · FR · EN** at the top of the page, and the
installer remembers your choice. Terms that come from Cloudflare or your
login provider (button names, OAuth terms) stay as those services show them.

### New at start.kaboutersoft.be
These aren't part of this release; they're already live:
- step-by-step guides for creating a Cloudflare account and the API token,
  and for moving your domain to Cloudflare to use your own address
  (Geavanceerd → Eigen domein);
- a language picker on every page;
- a privacy statement and terms of use for the demo.

## Updating
- Nothing to do: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. No
  database changes.
- Only the installer changed in this release; the other components are the
  same as in 0.1.14.
