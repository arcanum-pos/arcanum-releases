## What's new
- **Self-ordering: choose how guests pay.** In Evenementen → Instellen,
  each event now has two switches: **at the counter** (as before, on by
  default) and **with Bancontact on their phone**. With Bancontact, a guest
  taps "Betaal met Bancontact" after placing the order; the Bancontact app
  (or their bank's app) opens on the payment, and afterwards they're back
  on their order page, which shows it being prepared. If a payment fails,
  the page offers to try again — or to pay at the counter, when the event
  allows it. Bancontact needs to be set up first (Instellingen →
  Betalingen).
- **Self-ordering on a tab.** A third switch lets guests keep ordering on
  one tab and pay when they leave. Their phone shows "Je rekening" with the
  total so far, a button to order more, and how to settle up: at the
  counter with their number, or with Bancontact. Station screens make these
  orders straight away, marked "Op rekening". Orders stay anonymous, so
  there's no check that everyone pays before leaving.
- Station screens: a tap on an order can no longer be undone for a moment
  by a list that was still loading.

## Updating
- Update as usual: the installer updates itself first, then Arcanum. This
  release adds to the database (applied by the installer).
- Reload the kassas and customer displays after updating, so they show the
  new order numbers and the follow-your-order QR.
- Refunds aren't in Arcanum yet: if a guest paid with Bancontact and needs
  money back, do it in Bancontact's merchant portal.
